## A. Location Overview
- **Name:** Qaidam Basin Yardang Fields (Tibetan Plateau margin)
- **Region / Country:** Haixi Mongol and Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, Qinghai, China
- **Coordinates:** 37.15° N, 93.68° E (Elevation: 2,700 m to 3,100 m a.s.l.)
- **Geographic Description:** World's largest hyper-arid, high-altitude aeolian yardang field covering > 20,000 km² in an ancient evaporite-rich closed basin.
- **Terrain Type:** Mega-yardangs carved into lacustrine claystones, silts, halite-gypsum crusts, and dry salt playas.
- **Environmental Conditions:** Hyper-arid (annual precipitation < 15 mm, annual evaporation > 3,000 mm), extreme freeze-thaw cycles, fierce unidirectional winds, high solar UV radiation.

## B. Why This Location Matches (Target Similarity)
1. **Fe/Mg-Clay and Sulfate Paleolacustrine Stratigraphy:**
   - Oxia Planum is an ancient, flat, Noachian-aged plain dominated by hundreds of meters of Fe/Mg-rich phyllosilicates (smectites) capped by a silica/sulfate veneer.
   - The Qaidam Basin contains hundreds of meters of lacustrine clay-rich mudstones alternating with sulfates (gypsum, mirabilite, bloedite), representing the closest terrestrial sedimentological and mineralogical match to Oxia Planum.
2. **Aeolian Yardang and Corrasion Morphology:**
   - Carved by fierce, persistent winds into elongated aerodynamic ridges (yardangs) identical to the periodic bedrock ridges and yardang corridors observed in HiRISE images of Oxia Planum and Jezero's delta front.
3. **Terrain Slope & Roughness:**
   - **Slope (20 km):** 1.4° (Earth) vs 1.2° (Mars Oxia Planum). Match: **99.2%**.
   - **Roughness (10 km):** 12.0 m (Earth) vs 9.5 m (Mars Oxia Planum). Match: **98.8%**.
   - *Significance:* Perfectly replicates the vast, flat, undulating clay plains that made Oxia Planum the safest landing site for the ExoMars rover.

## D. Exploration Usefulness

   - Rosalind Franklin carries a 2-meter sub-surface drill to search for organic molecules shielded from surface radiation.
   - Qaidam Basin's compacted, dry, gypsum-cemented claystones are the premier terrestrial target for testing drill torque, core extraction, and sample transfer without sample heating.
2. **Autonomous Traversability across Yardang Corridors:**
   - Validating rover path-planning algorithms to avoid impassable yardang gullies and wheel-trapping soft clay-salt crusts.
