## 2. Key Discoveries from the Multi-Feature Engine

1. **Surfacing Overlooked Analogues:**
   - Traditional planetary analogue research repeatedly relies on a handful of well-known sites: McMurdo Dry Valleys, Devon Island, Death Valley, and the Atacama Desert.
   - The TERRATWIN quantitative multi-feature engine reveals that **Puna Plateau Volcanic Barren (Argentina)** and **Qaidam Basin Yardangs (China)** achieve significantly higher mathematical similarity (83.1% and 82.8%) to Jezero Crater and Oxia Planum than the famous sites.
   - Puna Plateau provides an unmatched analogue for combined high-UV, extreme diurnal swings, basaltic blockfields, and alkaline clay-carbonate paleolakes.
   - Qaidam Basin reproduces the exact sedimentological stratigraphy (Fe/Mg smectite clays capped by sulfates) and yardang erosion corridors of Oxia Planum.

2. **Mission Preset Specialization:**
   - No single Earth site is universally optimal for every mission phase.
   - For **Rover Mobility (terramechanics)**: Death Valley and McMurdo Dry Valleys score highest (98.7% and 96.2%), replicating wheel slip on steep scree and delta gravels.
   - For **Cold-Trap Volatile Prospecting**: Beacon Valley Permafrost leads (79.9%), uniquely possessing a dry, desiccated lag covering ancient buried ice.
   - For **Thermal Cycling Stress**: Puna Plateau leads (79.5%), providing 30 °C daily temperature swings at 4,200 m elevation.

3. **Rigorous Accounting of Physical Limits ("Where the Analogy Breaks Down"):**
   - Every site report explicitly accounts for Earth's 1.0 g gravity, 101.3 kPa nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere, and geomagnetic radiation shielding, preventing unrealistic mission extrapolations.
